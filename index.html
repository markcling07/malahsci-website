A college built by and for the people of Malapatan

Malapatan College of Science and Technology (MalahSCI) is the local college of the Municipality of Malapatan, Sarangani Province. Established in 2024, it gives young Malapateños the chance to earn a college degree without having to leave their hometown.

Supported by the Local Government Unit of Malapatan, MalahSCI offers free tuition and a growing set of programs in business, tourism, information systems, agribusiness, and social work, all aimed at helping every graduate stand on their own and serve their community.

Accessible Education

Free tuition and an affordable local campus, so finances and distance are no longer a barrier to a degree.

Science & Technology

Programs designed around the skills and industries that matter to Sarangani's growing economy.

Service to Community

Students, faculty, and staff serve side by side with the LGU, from relief operations to community outreach.

Academics

Degree Programs

Four-year bachelor's programs offered at MalahSCI.

BSE

BS in Entrepreneurship

Build, launch, and manage your own business with skills in marketing, finance, and innovation.

BSMA

BS in Management Accounting

Learn accounting, costing, and financial planning to support decision-making in organizations.

BSTM

BS in Tourism Management

Prepare for careers in travel, hospitality, and events while promoting Sarangani's destinations and culture.

BSIS

BS in Information Systems

Use technology to solve business problems through systems analysis, databases, and software.

BSAB

BS in Agribusiness

Combine agriculture and business to grow farm enterprises and strengthen local food supply chains.

BSSW

BS in Social Work

Train to help individuals, families, and communities through field exposure with social welfare agencies.

Admissions

How to Enroll

Open to incoming first-year students, transferees, and returning students.

  1. 1

    Check announcements

    Watch our official Facebook page for enrollment dates, available slots, and your program's schedule.

  2. 2

    Prepare requirements

    Bring the documents listed in the enrollment guidelines for your program (new or transferee).

  3. 3

    Visit the campus

    Enroll on your program's scheduled day with the Program Head and the Registrar's Office.

  4. 4

    Start your journey

    Get your ID, attend orientation, and enjoy 100% free tuition at MalahSCI.
